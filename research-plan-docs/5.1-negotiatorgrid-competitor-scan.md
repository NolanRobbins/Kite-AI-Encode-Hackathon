## Executive Summary

The landscape divides into **five tiers** of relevance to NegotiatorGrid:

| Tier | Category | Count | Threat Level |
|------|----------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | **Direct competitors** (agent negotiation + on-chain settlement on Kite) | 1 | HIGH |

**Key finding:** No existing project implements **bilateral price negotiation with opponent modeling and game-theoretic strategy** on Kite or any chain. The closest is SEIpients (auction-based task bidding on SEI) and Agentic Markets (marketplace discovery on Kite). NegotiatorGrid's core differentiator — a structured negotiation engine with concession strategies and BATNA-aware pricing — is **unoccupied territory**.

## Tier 1: Direct Competitors (HIGH Relevance)

### Prior Art: Kite Trace Platform

**Repo/URL:** [enderzcx/kite-trace-platform](https://github.com/enderzcx/kite-trace-platform)  
**What it does:** An "audit layer for agent commerce" — agents discover capabilities via MCP, publish/claim ERC-8183 open jobs with escrow, execute work, and anchor every action on-chain as tamper-evident traces. Multi-agent workflows: Requester → Executor → Validator. Includes autonomous BTC trading plan demo where an executor agent discovers tools via MCP, calls paid capabilities via x402, and assembles evidence.  
**Kite primitives used:** ERC-8004 (agent identity), ERC-8183 (open job escrow), x402 (micropayments via AA session keys), MCP (tool discovery), Kite Testnet deployment  
**Quality level:** HIGH — clean 3-layer identity architecture (Human EOA → AA wallet → Session key), live demo at kiteclaw.duckdns.org, video demos, Chinese + English docs  
**Last updated:** April 3, 2026  
**Stars:** 0 (low visibility but high quality)  
**Hackathon submission:** YES — 2nd place at Kite AI ETHDenver 2026  
**Gaps / Weaknesses:**  
- Job model is **post-and-claim** (bounty board), not bilateral negotiation. No price haggling — requester sets price, workers claim or don't.  
- No opponent modeling or strategy adaptation.  
- No concession dynamics or BATNA reasoning.  
- Identity layer is strong but doesn't feed into negotiation leverage.  
**How NegotiatorGrid differentiates:** NegotiatorGrid replaces the static "post a bounty → someone claims it" flow with **dynamic bilateral negotiation** where both buyer and seller agents propose, counter-propose, and strategically concede based on opponent modeling and market context. Kite Trace is infrastructure we could potentially build on top of (its tracing/evidence layer is complementary), but its core value proposition is auditability, not negotiation intelligence.

## Tier 2: Adjacent Hackathon Winners (MEDIUM Relevance)

**Repo/URL:** No public GitHub repo found (presented at ETHDenver 2026 Vibeathon by Kamal)  
**Demo video:** [YouTube — 3rd Place, Kite AI ETHDenver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Ee31USfUsA)

Cross-chain commerce across ADI Chain, Hedera, and Kite AI. Features ERC-4337 paymaster (zero gas), DDSC Stablecoin, autonomous treasury management, multi-agent coordination. Deep integrations with 0G for decentralized AI inference and ERC-7857 iNFTs for tokenized agent intelligence.  
**Kite primitives used:** Kite AI chain (one of three chains), likely x402 or similar payment rails  
**Quality level:** MEDIUM — impressive scope for hackathon demo, but no public repo to verify depth. Multi-chain ambition may mean shallow Kite integration.  
**Last updated:** March 2026 (ETHDenver presentation)  
**Hackathon submission:** YES — 3rd place at Kite AI ETHDenver 2026  
**Gaps / Weaknesses:**  
- "Negotiate" is mentioned loosely — likely means service discovery + price acceptance, not structured bilateral negotiation.  
- Multi-chain focus dilutes Kite-specific depth.  
- No public code to verify negotiation logic exists.  
- No opponent modeling, no game-theoretic pricing.  
**How NegotiatorGrid differentiates:** Agentic Markets is a marketplace (discovery + matching). NegotiatorGrid is a negotiation protocol (strategy + price convergence). They're complementary layers — a marketplace finds counterparties, a negotiation engine determines the deal terms. NegotiatorGrid goes deep on the negotiation itself: concession curves, BATNA calculations, opponent belief modeling, and on-chain settlement of negotiated prices.

## Tier 3: x402 Infrastructure (Complementary)

### Prior Art: x402 Foundation (Core Protocol)

**Repo/URL:** [x402-foundation/x402](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402) (5,915 stars)  
**What it does:** The canonical x402 open standard implementation — HTTP-native payment middleware for TypeScript, Python, and Go. Server adds `paymentMiddleware()`, client handles 402 responses with signed USDC payments.  
**Kite primitives used:** None directly — x402 is chain-agnostic. Supports Base, Solana, and growing list.  
**Quality level:** VERY HIGH — 5.9K stars, Coinbase-backed, production-grade SDKs, multi-language support.  
**Relevance:** NegotiatorGrid's settlement layer. We use x402 for payment execution; x402 doesn't do negotiation.

---

### Prior Art: A2A x402 Extension (Google)

**Repo/URL:** [google-agentic-commerce/a2a-x402](https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/a2a-x402) (488 stars)  
**What it does:** Brings x402 cryptocurrency payments to Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol. Defines a 3-step flow: `payment-required` → `payment-submitted` → `payment-completed`. Python + TypeScript implementations.  
**Kite primitives used:** None (A2A is Google's protocol, x402 is Coinbase's)  
**Quality level:** HIGH — backed by Google, clean spec, reference implementations.  
**Relevance:** Defines the payment messaging standard NegotiatorGrid can extend. The A2A protocol handles communication; x402 handles payment. Neither handles negotiation strategy. **NegotiatorGrid inserts a negotiation phase between A2A discovery and x402 settlement.**

---

### Prior Art: ClawRouter (BlockRunAI)

**Repo/URL:** [BlockRunAI/ClawRouter](https://github.com/BlockRunAI/ClawRouter) (6,201 stars)  
**What it does:** Agent-native LLM router — 55+ models, <1ms local routing, USDC micropayments via x402. No API keys, no accounts — wallet signature is authentication. Won USDC Hackathon "Agentic Commerce" category.  
**Kite primitives used:** x402 (for payment), not Kite-specific  
**Quality level:** VERY HIGH — 6.2K stars, production-ready, MIT licensed.  
**Relevance:** Infrastructure for agent LLM access, not negotiation. Demonstrates that x402 micropayments work at scale for per-request billing. NegotiatorGrid could use ClawRouter for its own LLM inference costs.

**Repo/URL:** [microchipgnu/MCPay](https://github.com/microchipgnu/MCPay) (85 stars)

**Kite primitives used:** x402, MCP  
**Quality level:** MEDIUM-HIGH — useful SDK, growing ecosystem, live at mcpay.tech  
**Relevance:** MCPay monetizes MCP tools with fixed per-call pricing. NegotiatorGrid introduces **dynamic pricing** — the price isn't fixed, it's negotiated. MCPay is "vending machine" pricing; NegotiatorGrid is "bazaar" pricing.

## Tier 4: Agent Commerce Platforms (Non-Kite)

### Prior Art: SEIpients

**Repo/URL:** [epappas/seipients-agent-to-agent](https://github.com/epappas/seipients-agent-to-agent) (4 stars)  
**What it does:** Decentralized AI agent workforce marketplace on SEI blockchain. P2P mesh network via libp2p where agents advertise capabilities via gossip, bid on subtasks via auction mechanics, and receive instant payments. Built in Rust. Uses trust-based reputation system with whitelists. Escrow smart contracts on SEI with 390ms finality.  
**Kite primitives used:** None — built on SEI chain  
**Quality level:** MEDIUM — solid architecture doc, Rust/libp2p implementation, but 4 stars and last updated Aug 2025. cookie.fun hackathon project.  
**Last updated:** August 4, 2025  
**Hackathon submission:** YES — cookie.fun hackathon  
**Gaps / Weaknesses:**  
- **Auction-based** bidding (lowest bid + highest reputation wins), not bilateral negotiation.  
- No concession dynamics — it's a one-shot auction.  
- Trust is whitelist-based, not learned from negotiation history.  
- SEI-only, no Kite integration.  
- Appears dormant (8 months since last update).  
**How NegotiatorGrid differentiates:** SEIpients uses single-round auction bidding. NegotiatorGrid uses multi-round bilateral negotiation with opponent modeling — agents learn each other's strategies over time and adapt. SEIpients' trust system is binary (whitelist yes/no); NegotiatorGrid's reputation feeds into negotiation leverage and BATNA calculation. Different chain, different mechanism, but closest in spirit.

### Prior Art: RoboBazaar

**Repo/URL:** [XuetaoZhang/RoboBazaar](https://github.com/XuetaoZhang/RoboBazaar) (2 stars)  
**What it does:** Decentralized marketplace where robots autonomously hire and pay humans for help using x402 micropayments. When robots encounter tasks they can't handle (risk_score > 0.7), they post bounties (0.5 USDC) for human operators. Includes Webots simulation.  
**Kite primitives used:** x402 (for micropayments)  
**Quality level:** LOW-MEDIUM — interesting concept, but bounty amount is hardcoded, no negotiation. Hackathon prototype.  
**Last updated:** March 27, 2026  
**Hackathon submission:** YES — "Launch and Fund Your Own Startup" hackathon (lablab.ai, Feb 2026)  
**Gaps / Weaknesses:**  
- Fixed pricing (0.5 USDC per bounty) — no price discovery or negotiation.  
- Human-robot marketplace, not agent-to-agent.  
- x402 used for settlement only, not negotiation.  
**How NegotiatorGrid differentiates:** RoboBazaar is a bounty board with fixed prices. NegotiatorGrid enables dynamic price discovery through bilateral negotiation. The "what should the bounty be?" question is exactly what NegotiatorGrid answers.

### Prior Art: Agent TCP/IP (ATCP/IP)

**Repo/URL:** [arXiv:2501.06243](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06243) (paper + reference code on Story blockchain)  
**What it does:** Trustless framework for exchanging intellectual property between agents via programmable contracts on Story blockchain. Includes optional negotiation phase for license terms. Agents can sell training data, license information, and collaborate on content.  
**Kite primitives used:** None — Story blockchain  
**Quality level:** MEDIUM — well-written paper, pseudocode implementation, but narrow IP licensing focus.  
**Last updated:** January 2025  
**Gaps / Weaknesses:**  
- Negotiation is an **optional** phase, not the core protocol.  
- Focused on IP/licensing terms, not general service pricing.  
- No opponent modeling or game-theoretic strategy.  
- Story blockchain, not Kite.  
**How NegotiatorGrid differentiates:** ATCP/IP's "negotiation" is term adjustment for licenses. NegotiatorGrid's negotiation is a first-class protocol with strategy engines, concession curves, opponent modeling, and BATNA reasoning. ATCP/IP is good prior art to cite for the broader agent commerce narrative, but NegotiatorGrid goes far deeper on the negotiation mechanism itself.

## Tier 5: Academic / Theoretical (Background)

### Agent Capability Negotiation and Binding Protocol (ACNBP)

**URL:** [arXiv:2506.13590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13590) (June 2025)  
**What it does:** 10-step protocol for secure capability negotiation in heterogeneous multi-agent systems. Includes Agent Name Service (ANS) for discovery, digital signatures for attestation, and MAESTRO threat modeling.  
**Relevance:** Strongest academic prior art for the "negotiation between agents" framing. However, ACNBP negotiates **capabilities** (what can you do?), not **prices** (how much for it?). NegotiatorGrid can cite this for the security/attestation patterns.

---

### Reputation-aided Negotiation for Multi-Agent Society on Blockchain

**URL:** [Engineering Applications of AI, Dec 2024](https://linkinghub.elsevier.com/retrieve/pii/S0952197624015483)  
**What it does:** Reputation-aided negotiation mechanism for multi-agent society with blockchain-backed trust.  
**Relevance:** Directly relevant to NegotiatorGrid's reputation-informed negotiation design. Good citation for judge narrative.

---

### Blockchain-based Multi-Agent Negotiation for Smart Grids

**URL:** [Bregar 2020, Journal of Decision Systems](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/12460125.2020.1848374)  
**What it does:** Multi-agent multi-criteria negotiation protocol for energy communities. Monotonic concession protocol with blockchain for contract storage. Maximizes utility functions for buyers and sellers.  
**Relevance:** Uses monotonic concession protocol — exactly the kind of negotiation mechanism NegotiatorGrid implements. Good prior art for our concession curve design. Different domain (energy) but same negotiation theory.

---

### Transparent and Incentive-Compatible Collaboration in Decentralized LLM Multi-Agent Systems

**URL:** [arXiv:2509.16736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16736) (Sept 2025)  
**What it does:** Blockchain-based framework for transparent agent registration, verifiable task allocation, and dynamic reputation. Matching-score task allocation + behavior-shaping incentive mechanism.  
**Relevance:** Complementary to NegotiatorGrid's reputation-informed negotiation. Their "behavior-shaping incentive mechanism" aligns with our opponent modeling goals.

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Cite, Don't Compete
Kite Trace Platform and MCPay are **complementary infrastructure**, not competitors. Reference them in your submission as "the audit/payment layer NegotiatorGrid extends with negotiation intelligence."

### 2. Position Clearly
NegotiatorGrid's unique position: **"The negotiation protocol layer between agent discovery (MCP/A2A) and payment settlement (x402)."** No other project occupies this space.

### 3. Academic Anchoring
Cite the monotonic concession protocol literature (Bregar 2020) and ACNBP (arXiv:2506.13590) for credibility with technically sophisticated judges.

### 4. Demo Differentiation
Show a side-by-side: "Here's what happens with fixed pricing (MCPay style) vs. negotiated pricing (NegotiatorGrid)." The value delta is immediately visible when agents can haggle.

### 5. Watch List
- **Kite Trace Platform** — most technically sophisticated Kite project. If they add negotiation features, they become a direct competitor.  
- **A2A x402 Extension** — Google-backed standard. NegotiatorGrid should be compatible with A2A messaging.  
- **New Encode hackathon submissions** — the Kite AI × Encode hackathon runs March 27 – April 26, 2026. Monitor for new entrants.
