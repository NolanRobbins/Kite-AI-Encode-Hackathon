# SOTP Divergence Screener

## Instructions
1. Act as a screener for an activist hedge fund. 
2. Screen for a mid-to-large cap tech or industrials company ($2B - $20B Market Cap) listed on the NYSE or NASDAQ that fits a 'Sum-of-the-Parts' (SOTP) profile. 
3. Search for companies where the legacy core business revenue is flat or declining (under 5% YoY growth), but they have a distinct, reported sub-segment (preferably in AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, data center cooling, or cloud networking) that is growing at 20%+ YoY. 
4. Output a table of 5 candidates with their total market cap, the name of the legacy segment, and the name/growth rate of the hidden growth segment.
